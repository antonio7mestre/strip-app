# Landing sticker assets

Created using the built-in image-generation tool, September 21, 2026.
The original Cosmos photos are documented separately in SOURCES.md.
These are generated objects, not extracts of the people in those photos.

All generated assets are resized for web delivery without flattening transparency. Each appears once in the landing collage.

## sticker-camera.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: isolated borderless photographic sticker for the Strip website collage. Primary request: A complete vintage compact point-and-shoot 35mm camera, silver aluminum face, black grips, round glass lens, tiny orange shutter detail, no wrist strap. Front three-quarter view with a small natural tilt. Real worn pocket camera, not a toy, not a vector illustration.
Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent alpha background, no backdrop, no ground plane. Composition: one complete object centered, modest 6% transparent padding, nothing clipped or cut off. All edges extremely clean and precisely extracted with no background remnants. Style: photorealistic tactile analog scrapbook cutout, direct daylight, slight film grain, playful editorial not luxury. Constraints: no white sticker rim, no border, no outline, no surrounding paper, no shadow outside the silhouette, no halo, no text, no logo, no watermark, no extra objects, no checkerboard baked into image. Output a transparent PNG.

Final edge-cleanup prompt:

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied camera image. Keep the exact photographed object and all of its details, texture, shape, color, and position. Remove ALL background pixels and the entire hazy glow around and between the object parts. The output must have a genuinely transparent alpha background with ZERO halo, haze, rim, outline, drop shadow or remnants. Trace the physical edges accurately. For goggles, the open space between the strap and lenses must be fully transparent. Keep the complete object in frame with modest transparent margins. Do not redesign, do not add anything. This is a clean photographic cutout sticker, no white border.
